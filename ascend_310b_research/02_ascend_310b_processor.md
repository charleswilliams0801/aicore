# 2. DaVinci V300 AI processor

The processor to look up is **Huawei Ascend 310B (昇腾310B)**. The [patent publication CN122884548A](https://eureka.patsnap.com/patent/CN122884548A) calls it HI1910 and identifies the V300 AI Core inside it. Huawei's [Atlas 200I A2 driver development guide](https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/Atlas%20200I%20A2/23.0.RC3/RC/driverdevelopmentguide/Atlas%20200I%20A2%20%E5%8A%A0%E9%80%9F%E6%A8%A1%E5%9D%97%2023.0.RC3%20%E9%A9%B1%E5%8A%A8%E5%BC%80%E5%8F%91%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97%2003.pdf) references hi1910B in a kernel device-tree include. This is evidence of the software platform identifier. A full SKU-to-package mapping requires a chip marking or vendor part-number document.

[Huawei's development-board spec](https://www.hiascend.com/developer/devboard/spec) lists 8 and 20 INT8 TOPS for Orange Pi AIpro variants. Use the [Huawei DK A2 hardware guide](https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/Atlas200IDKA2DeveloperKit/23.0.RC1/Hardware%20Interfaces/hiug/%E7%A1%AC%E4%BB%B6%E6%8E%A5%E5%8F%A3%E4%BD%BF%E7%94%A8%E6%8C%87%E5%8D%97.pdf) for board integration. No public standalone silicon datasheet with package, pinout and electrical limits was verified.
